Grammar cheat sheet

Its vs. It's

"Its" shows possession (the dog wagged its tail). "It's" is short for "it is" or "it has."

Their / There / They're

"Their" = possession. "There" = a place. "They're" = "they are."

Comma before "and" in a list

Use a comma before the final "and" in a list of three or more items (the Oxford comma): apples, oranges, and bananas.

Subject-verb agreement

Singular subjects take singular verbs: "She runs." Plural subjects take plural verbs: "They run."
